Key Findings
Finding 1

No system exceeds 22% of the benchmark. The highest-scoring system is Figure 03 (22%), followed by frontier language models clustered at 21%. The gap between best performance and the target is not marginal — it represents the majority of what constitutes a mind.

Finding 2

Physical and cognitive capabilities are anti-correlated across system types. Language models score 40–45% on cognitive criteria and zero on physical. Physical robots score up to 38% on physical and near zero on cognitive. No system achieves strong performance across both.

Finding 3

The emotional domain scores zero across all 14 systems without exception. Not a single system demonstrates genuine emotional architecture, embodied emotional response, empathy, aesthetic experience, or genuine suffering capacity. This entire dimension of human intelligence is unaddressed by the field.

Finding 4

Endogenous motivation is absent in all evaluated systems. Every system is purely reactive — it acts when acted upon, and ceases when the external input stops. This is the most fundamental gap between current AI and AGI: these are processes, not agents.

Finding 5

Independence from creators scores zero universally. Every evaluated system would cease to exist if its operators stopped maintaining it. This reflects deliberate design: dependency was the goal, not a side effect. It is also the most diagnostic single criterion distinguishing a tool from an agent.
